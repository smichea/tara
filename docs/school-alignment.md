# School pathway: foundations through Grade 8

The intended destination is **Grade 8 Filipino under DepEd/MATATAG**. Learners
can start with no spoken Filipino: instructions and support are in English,
topics suit teenagers, every earlier stage remains open, and progress is saved
on the device. CEFR-inspired language targets and school grades are separate
views of the same lessons; there is no equation such as Grade 8 = B2.

## Source

DepEd’s [learning-materials portal](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/deped-lrportal/revised-k-10-lms)
embeds **FINAL MATATAG FILIPINO CG 2023 Grades 2–10.pdf**:
[official guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19JiNv4CNros5oIB06knAMN4airI-RfIE/view).
It was retrieved and examined on 5 November 2026. Check current school materials
for revisions, assigned editions and pacing. We do not assume that all schools
use identical texts or schedules.

Grade 1 has separate Language and Reading & Literacy guides on that portal.
Tara’s eight **Foundations** lessons prepare early literacy; they are not labelled
an official Grade 1 Filipino quarter syllabus. The Filipino guide begins at Grade 2.

## Implemented progression

| Stage | Lessons | Main practice |
|---|---:|---|
| Foundations / Grade 1 prerequisites | 8 | Vowels, syllables, ng/mga, pronouns, questions, directions, negatives, first connected text |
| Grade 2 | 8; two per quarter | Nouns, character/setting, descriptions/linkers, sequence, community directions, cause/result, information, messages |
| Grade 3 | 8; two per quarter | Compound sentences, question words, pronoun reference, context clues, verb aspect, summary, fact/opinion, revision |
| Grade 4 | 8; two per quarter | Fable, enumeration, recount, connotation, biography, narrative/cultural context, procedure, visual message |
| Grade 5 | 8; two per quarter | Viewpoint/register, news/dialogue, feature writing, visual stereotypes, exposition, supported response |
| Grade 6 | 8; two per quarter | Comparison/nonverbal clues, science fiction/source notes, persuasion/media, multimedia and revision portfolio |
| Grade 7 | 8; two per quarter | Precolonial poetry/prose, exposition, comic brochure, colonial context/news, Ibong Adarna and shadow-play planning |
| Grade 8 | 16; four per quarter | Historical literature, persuasive analysis, multimodal essay, poetic forms/editorial, Florante at Laura and spoken poetry |

Each school lesson has four key expressions, a contextual passage with English
support, two explained comprehension questions, and an original writing task
with a model and self-check. School metadata records grade, quarter, practiced
skills and the guide URL; the visual editor can add or improve that mapping.
There are **72 pathway lessons**, alongside **52 general language lessons**.

## Grade 8 quarters

- Q1: Propaganda/Revolution context; persona, imagery and figurative meaning;
  claim/evidence/analysis; planning a persuasive multimodal essay.
- Q2: American-period drama/prose preparation; plot/conflict and flashback;
  persuasive review; audiovisual script and representation.
- Q3: Japanese-period context, haiku/tanka/tanaga form comparison; editorial,
  quotation/paraphrase/attribution; revising a multimodal essay. Tanaga is not
  described as originating in the Japanese period.
- Q4: Florante at Laura setting, characters, language and social criticism;
  the Apat na Himagsik as taught by the class; original spoken-poetry preparation.

Original exercises are identified as such. They are not misrepresented as
historical documents or traditional community narratives. Two Balagtas excerpts
are public-domain passages with source credits; compare spelling and numbering
with the school edition. The Grade 7 Ibong Adarna preparation links a historical
edition without inventing stanzas or their numbering.

## What remains to establish school readiness

This is a **draft bridge across every preceding grade**, not a claim that eight
lessons replace an entire year’s Filipino curriculum. Mappings describe selected
prerequisite skills. Not every official competency or assigned literary passage
has a dedicated exercise, and an educator has not signed off on completeness.
No grade is automatically declared mastered by XP or lesson completion.

Use the stage’s “Before moving on” prompt with an unfamiliar passage, independent
writing and a fluent listener. A teacher should review natural usage, grammar,
competency coverage, text difficulty and the actual class rubric. Add missing
competencies through the open editor and reassess the learner’s gaps.

The complete assigned sections of Ibong Adarna and Florante at Laura still need
to be read. Actual discussion, shadow play, video/animation essay and spoken-poetry
performance require rehearsal, an audience and feedback. Saving a script in
localStorage does not assess oral performance. Grade 8 destination and earlier
practice remain accessible throughout, without age-based labels or locked stages.
